## Clinical demo data

The seed includes examples of:

- Pending, confirmed, completed, cancelled and rejected appointments
- Completed medical records
- Requested, in-progress and completed laboratory tests
- Pending, processing and dispensed prescriptions
- Multiple medicines, including a low-stock example

All names, contact details and clinical information are fictional and intended only for demonstration.
